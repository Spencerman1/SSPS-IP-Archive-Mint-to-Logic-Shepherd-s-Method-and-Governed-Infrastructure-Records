### **1\. Text Data APIs**

#### **Purpose: Retrieve public domain or relevant textual content.**

* **Project Gutenberg API**:  
  * Access public domain texts for generating historical or literary content.  
  * **Documentation**: [https://www.gutenberg.org](https://www.gutenberg.org)  
* **Bible Gateway API**:  
  * Retrieve scripture and faith-based texts for specialized content.  
  * **Documentation**: [https://www.biblegateway.com](https://www.biblegateway.com)  
* **Open Library API**:  
  * Provides book metadata and access to public domain books.  
  * **Documentation**: https://openlibrary.org/developers/api

---

### **2\. Visual Data APIs**

#### **Purpose: Retrieve copyright-free images and videos.**

* **Pixabay API**:  
  * Search for royalty-free images and videos.  
  * **Documentation**: https://pixabay.com/api/docs/  
* **Wikimedia Commons API**:  
  * Access public domain and Creative Commons-licensed media.  
  * **Documentation**: [https://commons.wikimedia.org](https://commons.wikimedia.org)  
* **Unsplash API**:  
  * Retrieve high-quality free-to-use images for visual content.  
  * **Documentation**: https://unsplash.com/developers

* **USGS Earthquake API**:

  * **Documentation**: https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/
