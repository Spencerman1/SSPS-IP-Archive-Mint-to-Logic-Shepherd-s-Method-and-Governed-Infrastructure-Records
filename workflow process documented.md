The concept of a "poor man's copyright" — where you mail a copy of your work to yourself as evidence of creation date — is not a legally recognized form of copyright protection. In modern copyright law, particularly in countries that adhere to the Berne Convention (including the U.S.), copyright is automatically granted as soon as a work is fixed in a tangible medium (e.g., written, recorded, or otherwise documented).

### **Why "Poor Man's Copyright" Isn't Effective**

1. **No Legal Weight**: Mailing yourself a copy of your work provides no official proof in a court of law. A sealed, dated envelope may not hold up as evidence since it’s easy to tamper with or fake.  
2. **Modern Alternatives Are Better**: Today, digital timestamps and official copyright registration provide far stronger legal protection.

---

### **Practical and Low-Cost Alternatives to Protect Your Work**

#### **1\. Automatically Protected by Copyright Law**

* As soon as your workflow is documented (written, drawn, recorded), it is automatically copyrighted under U.S. law and similar laws in most countries.

#### **2\. Use Digital Timestamps**

* Services like **Google Drive**, **Dropbox**, or **emailing yourself** create timestamps that can serve as additional evidence of creation dates.  
* Platforms like **GitHub** can also document changes over time for workflows or software-related projects.

#### **3\. Register with the U.S. Copyright Office**

* **Cost**: $45–$65 for most works.  
* **Why**: Official registration provides the strongest protection and is required if you need to file a lawsuit.  
* **How**: Submit an application online at [copyright.gov](https://www.copyright.gov/), along with your work.

#### **4\. Creative Commons Licenses (Optional)**

* If you want to share your work publicly while maintaining control, use a **Creative Commons license**. This is free and allows you to set conditions for reuse (e.g., attribution required, no commercial use).

#### **5\. Blockchain Timestamping**

* Services like **Ascribe** or **Verisart** can create tamper-proof records of your work’s existence on the blockchain.  
* Some platforms are free or low-cost, and they provide cutting-edge verification.

### **Conclusion**

While a "poor man's copyright" might seem like a quick fix, it’s unreliable and unnecessary today.
